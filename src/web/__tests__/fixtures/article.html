The Tide & the Lighthouse

By Mara Ward · 12 March 2021

The keeper’s log is short, and most of it is weather. It runs to 3 × 40 pages, kept in a tin box (see the notes).

What the log says

Wind NNE, fresh. Lamp lit at 4.

Nothing passed.

Three things stand out:

  • The lamp was lit every night.
  • Supplies came by boat:
    1. oil
    2. flour & salt
  • Visitors were rare.

After 1950

The light was automated.
The log stops.
